Before you pack

What does the €1,490 include?

The 7-day package includes your private apartment and studio access for up to three guests, a studio walkthrough, and airport pickup and drop-off. Flights are not included. Optional production or engineering support are extra. We’ll confirm your dates, arrival arrangements and the full booking details before you commit.

Is that per person or for the whole group?

€1,490 is the total package price for up to three guests. When three people share, that’s under €500 each for the full 7 days. Solo artists and duos are welcome too, with adjusted package rates available depending on availability.

Can more than 3 people come?

Yes, we can accommodate up to 6 guests. The retreat package includes up to 3 people; additional guests cost €40 per person, per night.

Can we use the studio independently?

Yes. After a walkthrough and introduction to the setup, you can work independently. Tell us what you plan to record so we can discuss the equipment and any support you need.

Do we need to bring an engineer?

You can bring your own engineer, work independently after the walkthrough, or book additional support. Optional engineering is €150 for a half day (4 hours) or €280 for a full day (8 hours). Multi-day support is quoted for your project.

Can I come to write rather than record?

Absolutely. The retreat suits songwriting, production, rehearsing ideas and finishing existing tracks as well as recording new material. Tell us what you want to work on.

How do we get there?

Fly to Gran Canaria Airport (LPA). Airport pickup and drop-off are included; the studio is approximately 30 minutes from the airport. We’ll arrange the details with you before arrival. Direct routes and flight schedules vary by season.

How do I check winter availability?

Send your preferred dates, project name and email using the form below, or chat with us on WhatsApp. We’ll reply to confirm availability and discuss your plans. Sending an enquiry does not take payment or confirm a booking.
