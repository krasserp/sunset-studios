Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This notice covers https://sunset-studios.one/, our retreat and recording studio website, and https://music.sunset-studios.one/, our music shop. Sunset Studios is based in Gran Canaria, Spain.

Who handles your information

Sunset Studios is responsible for the information you provide when enquiring about our services or purchasing from our music shop. For privacy questions or requests, contact phil@sunset-studios.one.

Enquiries and bookings

Our availability form asks for your preferred dates, band or project name, booking type (retreat, recording or rehearsal) and email address. We use these details to reply, check availability and discuss your booking. The required fields help us assess your request; you can contact us by email instead of using the form. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your message.

Form submissions are sent to Formspree, which processes and stores enquiries and delivers them to us. Formspree may also process technical information such as your IP address and browser details for service operation and security. See Formspree’s privacy policy.

If you choose WhatsApp, your phone number, profile information and messages are processed through WhatsApp to discuss your enquiry. You can use the form or email instead. See WhatsApp’s privacy policy.

If you email us directly, our email service processes your address and correspondence so that we can respond.

We process booking enquiries to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and booking arrangements to perform that contract. For general correspondence and service security, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to requests and operating the studio. We may also retain records where needed to meet legal obligations or handle legal claims.

Music purchases and payments

When you shop at music.sunset-studios.one, we process the contact details, billing information, items purchased, order history and payment status needed to fulfil your order, deliver digital music, provide purchase support and maintain required records.

Payments are processed through Stripe. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider; we do not store full card details on our servers. Stripe processes payment and technical information to complete transactions, help prevent fraud and meet its legal obligations. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

We use order information to perform the purchase contract and retain relevant transaction records to meet legal and accounting obligations. We may also use relevant records to resolve payment disputes and protect against fraud, based on our legitimate interests in operating the shop securely.

The shop may use cookies or similar storage to maintain a shopping cart or checkout session and support payment security. Payment providers may also use their own cookies or identifiers during checkout. Browser restrictions on these technologies may affect checkout functionality.

Website measurement and cookies on the studio site

If you accept analytics cookies, the studio website at sunset-studios.one loads Google Tag Manager, which manages measurement tags. With analytics enabled, after the form service accepts an enquiry, our form code sends a form_submitted event to Tag Manager with the form identifier and project type. This helps measure enquiries and, when connected to analytics, understand which channels bring visitors to the site.

This event does not include your project name, email address or preferred dates. Tags configured in Tag Manager may process other usage information, such as pages visited, referral information, device details and online identifiers, and may use cookies. The cookies and data collected depend on the tags enabled in the container.

You can use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. On the studio site, use the cookie icon or Cookie settings in the footer to accept, reject or change optional analytics and embedded content preferences. Both are off until you choose. The settings panel opens automatically on your first visit or when your saved choice expires. You can reopen it at any time. Your choice is saved for up to 180 days in a necessary cookie named sunset-cookie-consent-v1, scoped to sunset-studios.one and its subdomains. This allows the music shop to use the same preferences once its consent controls are connected to this cookie. Withdrawing analytics consent reloads the page to stop loaded tags. Previously stored third-party cookies can be removed through your browser settings. For information about Google’s processing, see Google’s privacy policy.

Maps, videos and external links

Some pages embed Google Maps or YouTube content. Embeds on the studio site load only after you enable embedded content in Cookie settings. When an embed loads, your browser connects to Google, which can receive technical information such as your IP address and may use cookies or other identifiers under Google’s privacy policy.

Links to Instagram, TikTok and other external websites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Service providers and international processing

Information may be handled by our website hosting and email providers, Formspree, Stripe, and Google where their services are used. These services support website delivery, enquiry handling, order fulfilment, payments, security and measurement.

Providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Formspree states that its infrastructure includes the United States. Their privacy policies explain their international processing practices; you can contact us with questions about providers handling your enquiry.

How long information is kept

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to answer your request and manage any resulting booking. We keep order and transaction records as needed to fulfil purchases, provide support and meet applicable accounting and tax obligations. Relevant records may be kept longer where needed for legal obligations or disputes. Copies held in email and Formspree are also subject to the applicable account settings and provider retention practices. Contact us if you would like us to review or delete your enquiry records.

Your choices and rights

Under applicable data protection law, you may request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or a portable copy, and may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send requests to phil@sunset-studios.one. We may need to verify your identity before providing information. You may also complain to Spain’s Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD) or your local data protection authority.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when our website or data handling changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
