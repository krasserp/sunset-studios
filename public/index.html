FAQ

What is Sunset Studios?

Sunset Studios is a creative retreat in Gran Canaria with a fully equipped soundproof studio and accommodation for artists, songwriters, and producers who want time and space to focus and create.

Is it more for recording or writing sessions?

Both. It’s ideal for writing and recording.

How many people can stay?

The guest apartment can host up to three people. Additional accommodation may be available on request for up to 3 more guests.

Can I rent the apartment and the studio together?

Yes. The apartment is connected to the studio, and the standard package includes both. Local artists may also rent the studio separately on occasion.

Do I need to bring my own engineer?

No. Engineering support is available if needed, and you’re also welcome to bring your own engineer.

Can we use the studio on our own?

Yes. After a walkthrough and brief introduction, you can use the studio independently.

Where is Sunset Studios located?

Sunset Studios is in Gran Canaria, Canary Islands, Spain — surrounded by nature and close to the coast.
