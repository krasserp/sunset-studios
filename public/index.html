FAQ

What is Sunset Studios?

Sunset Studios is a creative retreat in Gran Canaria with a soundproof fully equipped studio and accommodation options for artists, songwriters and producers who want time and space to create.

Is it more for recording or writing sessions?

Both. The studio is ideal for writing sessions and recording.

How many people can stay?

The guest apartment can host up to 3 people. Additional guest rooms may also be available.

Can I rent the apartment and the studio together?

Yes. The apartment is joint to the studio and the standard package is appartment + the studio. Local artists rent the studio on occassions seperately.

Do I need to bring my own engineer?

No. Engineering assistance can be provided if needed. You're welcome to bring your engineer if desired.

Can we use the studio on our own?

Yes. After a proper walkthrough and demo, guests can use the equipment themselves.
